# BuildFlow Pro — Design Bible v1.0

## Product Identity

BuildFlow Pro is a Construction Tender & Project Communication System.

It is not a PowerPoint skin or generic construction template. It is positioned as an internal communication system inspired by top-tier engineering consultancies, helping construction, engineering, and property firms communicate credibility, speed, and structure.

## Design Philosophy

Engineering Editorial Minimalism.

Combination:
- Engineering precision: grids, technical diagrams, restrained colors, structured data.
- Editorial confidence: white space, strong typography, professional pacing.

Reference feeling:
- Arup capability documents
- AECOM annual reports
- McKinsey consulting presentations

Avoid:
- orange/black construction clichés
- generic stock worker photos
- gradients and glassmorphism
- futuristic AI visuals
- decorative fonts
- excessive accent colors

## Color System

Primary:

Charcoal #20252B
- text
- dark backgrounds
- structural lines

Concrete Gray #E7E5DF
- cards
- dividers
- secondary backgrounds

Warm White #F8F7F3
- default background

Accent:

Blueprint Blue #2D5B84
- primary accent
- diagrams
- icons
- links

Safety Amber #D89B2B
- warnings and attention states only

Quality Green #54705B
- approved / positive states

Risk Red #B64B45
- risks and negative states

Rule:
Accent colors must communicate meaning, never decoration.

## Typography

Primary:
Inter

Secondary:
IBM Plex Sans

Hierarchy:
- Cover title: Inter Bold
- Section title: Inter SemiBold
- Slide title: Inter SemiBold
- Body: IBM Plex Sans Regular
- KPI numbers: Inter

Maximum 4 text styles per slide.

## Layout System

Canvas:
16:9 presentation format

Grid:
12-column system.

Rules:
- consistent margins
- strong alignment
- top header band
- footer system
- spacing based on modular scale

Cards:
- minimal radius
- subtle border
- clean engineering appearance

Diagrams:
Must remain editable using native PowerPoint/Figma shapes.

## Icon System

Style:
Blueprint technical monoline icons.

Rules:
- consistent stroke
- orthographic view
- no 3D perspective
- no unnecessary detail

Example categories:
- crane
- excavator
- blueprint
- concrete
- steel structure
- safety
- inspection
- quality control
- schedule
- procurement

## Illustration System

Style:
Editorial Vector Duotone.

Characteristics:
- technical
- minimal
- professional
- semi-realistic proportions
- minimal facial detail

Use:
- architects
- engineers
- construction planning
- site management
- quality inspection
- safety briefing

Avoid:
- cartoon style
- photorealistic AI people
- excessive colors

## Diagram Rules

Preferred diagrams:
- process flows
- timelines
- risk matrices
- KPI dashboards
- lifecycle diagrams

Rules:
- simple hierarchy
- editable components
- clear labels
- technical presentation quality

## Product MVP Structure

BuildFlow Pro MVP contains:

1. Company Capability
2. Project Understanding
3. Construction Methodology

4. Site Management
5. HSE System
6. QA/QC System
7. Project Control

Total target:
approximately 60 slides.

## Quality Standard

Every output must feel like:
"A professional engineering consultancy communication system."

Never:
"An AI-generated construction template."
